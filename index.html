Twitch Description:

Trevor D. Tesch, known professionally as AuthTrevor, is an American streamer and cybersecurity enthusiast. He focuses more on developing code and producing beats for himself, and he also wanted to act. Born in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, in 2013, he spent most of his childhood living there until he was 10, when he moved to Raleigh, North Carolina. Then he got a career on Twitch. He stated, “I want to connect with people who like me and inspire,” said in a private interview when he was only 13. He continues to create more content and engagement to his viewers and fanbase. He developed and coded mainly to get around his school restrictions; he was the first to stream real Minecraft in school.
