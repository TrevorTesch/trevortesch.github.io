Twitch Description:

Trevor D Tesch, professionally known as AuthTrevor, is an American streamer and cybersecurity enthusiast. He does more developing code and producing beats for himself, and he wanted to act. Born in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, in 2013, he spent most of his childhood living there until he was 10, when he moved to Raleigh, North Carolina. Then he got a career on Twitch. After all, he needed some side money because he said, “My chores aren’t enough; I want to connect with people who like me and need to make more money at a young age,” said in a questioning from a private interview when he was only 13. He continues to create more content and engagement to his viewers for his fanbase. He developed and coded mainly to get around his school restrictions; he was the first to stream real Minecraft in school.
